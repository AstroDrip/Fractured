# World Bible

## Central truth
Reality is no longer reliable, but the world is still tangible enough to fear. The
catastrophe has already happened. The protagonist — a slender hooded teenager who
remembers language and basic actions but not their name — learns the world by walking it.

## Realities (internal names; the player is never told them)
- **Normal** — the reference: stable, readable, quietly wrong at the edges.
- **Void** — absence of matter. What remains is held up by the flame. Spans that exist only in absence can be walked here. The flame burns down.
- **Deranged** — contradiction. Geometry disagrees with itself, light comes from below, time runs backward for some things, and near the white wall gravity turns toward it.

## Rules
1. A held phase leads to the local fracture's reality: Void on the near side of the gap, Deranged beyond it.
2. Phasing requires the flame. The Void cannot be held without it; when the flame goes out there, the Void ends.
3. What you stand on must exist in the reality you are entering; otherwise the world refuses (strain). On the wall, the world refuses to let you leave Deranged.
4. Losing the ground underfoot returns you to where that piece began. Nothing kills the protagonist; a creature can throw them back.
5. Matter removed by the Void still casts its shadow.
6. "Never let the flame go out in the dark." — remembered, not explained.

## The flame
Light, perception (reveals hidden spans, draws drifters, finds anomalies), survival
resource (fuel), and a restrained offensive tool (an ember or pulse drives the Hound off
and scatters drifters). It is the same colour as the signal lamp and the moving light.

## Beings
| Being | Class | Behaviour | Reality |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Watcher | avoidant | tall figure at the tree line; retreats when approached or lit; relocates ahead only unobserved | vanishes during transitions; a trace in Void |
| Drifters | flame-sensitive | small cube-bodied things drawn toward the flame; scatter on a pulse and re-form | visible in Void only near the flame, where they settle on the hidden bridge |
| Hound | pursuer | rests on the line past the wall; running, the open flame or a phase nearby wakes it; stalks, then pursues briefly; repelled by flame; throws the protagonist back | seen in skips in Deranged |
| The figure / Fractured Humanoid | presence | stands across the gap and fades as approached; at the end stands on the line beyond the buffer stop, against the light | — |

## Motifs
Huts that repeat (window broken, broken, whole); poles that compress and change sides;
footprints that stop, that return, that walk up a wall before anyone has; signs facing
the wrong reader; the amber colour shared by flame, lamp and light; the shadow that
outlives its object.

## Unresolved by design
Who the protagonist is; what the light is; who the figure is; who walked the wall first;
what holds the landmass; who keeps the lamp and the window lit. The priest arc, the pond,
the manifestation stages and the catastrophe's cause are out of scope and not hinted at
beyond silhouette.
