## Discoverability
Every required mechanic is hinted once, in context, and only when it applies: run (z 18),
fire (z 44, again at z 76), gather (after first summon), vault (at the pole), phase (at
the barrier; at the edge if not used), gather at the edge (after 3 s), walk into the wall
(once Deranged near it), sneak (when the Hound wakes). Refusals and falls carry their
own explanatory thought the first time.
